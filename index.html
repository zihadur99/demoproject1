Practicing Medicine with Real Heart, Clarity, and Maternal Understanding

Dr. Mariam Nasrallah-Moussa is a board-certified Family Medicine physician who chose this specialty because she believes the deepest, most effective medical care occurs when an entire family is cared for over time.

Between managing clinic consultations and raising her own young children, Dr. Mariam understands firsthand how exhausting medical jargon, rushed appointments, and disjointed care can be for busy parents and working families. She created this practice to bring back what medicine should always be: unhurried conversations, thoughtful diagnostics, and clear guidance you can actually act on.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear details about visit options, pricing, insurance, and medical policies.

We offer comprehensive in-person clinic visits (routine physicals, well-woman and well-child exams), same-day sick visits for acute issues, secure virtual telehealth appointments for follow-ups and consultations, and our Direct Medical Advice written inquiry channel for non-urgent guidance.

We believe in upfront, transparent pricing without hidden facility fees. While we operate primarily as a direct practice to ensure unhurried 30-to-60 minute visits, we supply all patients with detailed itemized receipts (superbills) that you can easily submit to your insurance carrier for out-of-network reimbursement. HSA and FSA cards are welcome.

Direct Medical Advice is designed for non-emergency inquiries—such as second opinions, clarifying lab tests, pediatric triage questions, or deciding whether symptoms require an in-person evaluation. Important: If you or a loved one is experiencing severe chest pain, sudden difficulty breathing, slurred speech, acute injury, or life-threatening symptoms, please call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.
